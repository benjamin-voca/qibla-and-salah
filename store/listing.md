# Connect IQ Store listing

## App name

**Qibla and Salah**

## Short description (one line)

Prayer times on your wrist, with a reminder before each prayer's time runs out.

## Description

Qibla and Salah tells you how much time is left for the current prayer and reminds
you before it runs out, so a busy day never quietly takes a prayer from you.

A crescent across the top of the watch face shrinks as the prayer's time passes.
It turns from green to amber to red as the end approaches, and a small notch marks
the moment you'll be reminded. Below it: a large countdown and the next prayers.
When you've prayed, tap the screen (or press the select button) and the crescent
turns gold. That prayer's reminder is cancelled.

REMINDERS THAT RESPECT YOUR DAY
• A warning before each prayer's time ends: 20 minutes by default, or any time
  from 5 to 90 minutes, separately for each prayer (or off)
• Optional alert when each prayer time begins
• On newer watches (Connect IQ 5.1+): real notifications with "Prayed" and
  "Not yet" buttons. "Not yet" reminds you again 5 minutes later
• On older watches: a gentle vibrating prompt

ACCURATE, OFFLINE, EVERYWHERE
• All times are calculated on the watch. It works in airplane mode, abroad, and
  without your phone
• The calculation method is chosen automatically from your location, from 23
  recognised authorities: Muslim World League, ISNA, Umm al-Qura (with the
  Ramadan Isha rule), Egyptian Authority, Karachi, Diyanet, Tehran, Shia
  Ithna-Ashari, Gulf, Kuwait, Qatar, Dubai, JAKIM, Kemenag, MUIS, UOIF, Russia,
  Tunisia, Algeria, Morocco, Portugal, Jordan, and the Islamic Community of
  Kosovo's takvim
• Standard or Hanafi Asr; Isha until Islamic midnight or until Fajr
• High-latitude handling (angle-based, middle of the night or one-seventh), and
  the Islamic Fiqh Council rule above 66°
• Extra minutes per prayer if your mosque adds precaution (ihtiyat)
• Daylight-saving changes are automatic

MORE THAN A TIMETABLE
• Today: Imsak, Fajr, Sunrise, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha and Islamic midnight
• Qibla compass: the needle points to the Kaaba, and the watch buzzes and turns
  green when you're facing it
• Hijri date (Umm al-Qura, adjustable ±2 days)
• Week view: which prayers you marked in the last six days, and your streak
• Glance: the current prayer and the time left, one swipe from your watch face

EASY TO SET UP
• Uses your watch's location, or pick from about 110 cities worldwide, or enter
  coordinates in the Garmin Connect app
• Everything can be changed on the watch: hold the lower button

Languages: English, العربية, Bosanski/Hrvatski, Deutsch, Français, Bahasa
Indonesia, Bahasa Melayu, Nederlands, Русский, Türkçe.

Free and open source (MIT). No ads, no account, no data collection:
nothing ever leaves your watch.

Source code, issues and feedback: https://github.com/benjamin-voca/qibla-and-salah

First release.

## Permissions (why the app needs them)

- Background: to wake up at reminder times.
- Positioning: to calculate times for where you are (never sent anywhere).
- Notifications: reminders with Prayed / Not yet buttons (Connect IQ 5.1+).
- Sensor: compass heading for the Qibla page.

## Privacy

The app has no network access and collects no data. Your location is only used
on the watch.
